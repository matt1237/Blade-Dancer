## Sword / Combat
- Sword state/rendering: `res://scripts/player.gd` (`BLADE_PROFILES`, `blade_angle`, `sword_phase`, `_update_sword()`)
- Swept collision: `res://scripts/sword_interaction_resolver.gd` (`SwordInteractionResolver.swept_contact`)
- BDOS collision-constrained sword bench: `res://scripts/bdos_sword_solver.gd` (`BDOSSwordSolver`), connected through `Player._advance_collision_sword_pose()` and the read-only `_sword_transform()`. `collision_sword_enabled` is a default-OFF, Hit-Reaction-master-gated per-contact-preset binary under **Physics Shells**, surfaced as **Collision Constrained Sword 2**. Persisted through `HitReaction.DEFAULTS` and existing preset authority. ON, legacy bone pose constraint, bite/glance/surface/shell pose offsets and the blade shell stand down; previous resolved hilt/angle and the same curved polyline feed rendering, trails and hit detection. Capsule-vs-actual enemy body circle (the shared enemy scene currently uses CircleShape2D radius 14) and enemy weapon capsule substeps project contact motion including angular leverage and constrain hand reach. Two-way contact: a pressed enemy body yields along the contact normal via `Enemy.receive_collision_sword_push(normal, amount)` — the per-frame resisted depenetration pressure the solver now exposes on its contacts, scaled by `COLLISION_SWORD_YIELD_GAIN` and the enemy's existing `grapple_weight` resistance (Light yields most, Heavy least). It is an ask through the existing knockback channel (terrain respected by move_and_slide); a committed windup/charging Turkey/Wolf/Ogre refuses, only the normal component pushes so tangential slide stays free, and there is no pose write, orbit, drag, or attach; `COLLISION_SWORD_YIELD_GAIN` is 0.5 so a pressed body is nudged, not shoved. A far side contact also BENDS the blade around the body: `_contact_correction` biases a lever-fraction past `BEND_AXIAL_LEVER_FRACTION` toward rotation about the hand (a `BEND_TRANSLATION_FLOOR` keeps the solve converging), while an axial thrust has no lever and still stops by translation. Perf: the solver hot path is now allocation-light (segment-closest pair allocates once instead of building a candidate array; `_bounded_step` returns a value type and morphs its buffer in place) and the projection budget was cut to the minimum the focused tests prove — 4 passes, down from 16. Solver cost is measured (never unconditional) via `Player.collision_sword_perf_debug` or `blade_bone_debug_enabled`, printing `[BDOS SOLVER PERF]` calls/total/avg/max microseconds every 60 solves. Solver-only live bench gives the player test-only invulnerability so hit-stagger cannot skip sword updates; game stagger is unchanged. No PX imports, damage authority, hand spring, terrain collision, or mutual enemy-sword solver. Finite solve budget reports incompatible overlaps via Bone Boundary Debug rather than promising guaranteed non-penetration in impossible geometry. Bench: `res://tests/bdos_collision_sword_rig.tscn` (real Main + fixed Turkey target, controller aim driven at the target, player invulnerable and Hilt Bash disabled only inside this solver test rig, in-memory settings only); focused tests: `res://tests/bdos_sword_solver_test.gd` (15 tests: geometry, reach, stagger, ON/OFF gating, inward-press vs tangential pass, weight resistance, immune refusal, crowded convergence).
